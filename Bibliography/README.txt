Traceroute of bibliography searches
===================================

- All articles are named after their citation

- The number before the name marks the "reference number path" from the first article "00"
(e.g. "30.41" means it is reference 41 of the article which is reference 30 of the article 00)

- "mod" mention after the number means it is a modified "annotated" version of the original article (which is also in the directory)
WARNING: the original article should be the only one to be kept for reference, the "mod" one is for local usage convenience only

- "bis" mention after the number means it is an article directly refering and answering to the original one.

Limitaion: this system works only because there is only one article sourcing all others,
	   as soon as other non-related references come up it should be revised.
